Your profile includes your name, role, and the context Mother Tree builds about your work over time. You don't need to fill anything in manually — it builds as you interact.

The more you engage — training sessions, signal captures, conversation debriefs — the better the personalisation. Early on, expect generic content. After a few weeks, it gets specific.

To check your current profile, DM me `status`. To see your training progress, say `progress`.
