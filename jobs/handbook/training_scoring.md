Every training response you give is assessed by three independent models, each scoring against the same criteria. If they agree, the result is automatic. If they disagree, a triage model reviews, and if needed, a senior arbitrator makes the final call.

This matters because a single model can be gamed. Multi-judge scoring is harder to flatter and more honest about gaps.

Scores are not grades — they're signals. The goal is to see patterns in your thinking and adjust what comes next in the curriculum.
