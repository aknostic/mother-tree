When a Pulse nudge arrives and the timing is wrong — you're mid-deal, the contact is in a sensitive period, or you just spoke to them yesterday — snooze it.

Reply to the nudge with `snooze` or `snooze 2 weeks`. I'll hold it and resurface it at the right time. The relationship clock doesn't reset on a snooze; it resets when you actually interact.

Snoozing is better than ignoring. Ignored nudges pile up and lose meaning. Snoozed nudges come back sharpened.
