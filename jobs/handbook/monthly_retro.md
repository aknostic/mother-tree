The monthly retrospective arrives on the first of each month. It's LLM-synthesised from the full record: interactions, training progress, signals captured, pipeline movement, and rhythm consistency.

The goal is pattern recognition at a cadence where patterns are visible. Weekly reviews are too granular. Monthly retrospectives show you whether you're moving in the right direction.

Read it, mark what resonates, and use it to set your intent for the month. It's a coaching conversation, not a performance review.
