Mother Tree combines a sales methodology with a live intelligence feed from your market. It runs in the background, surfacing what matters when it matters.

For hunters: it sharpens your conversations and keeps your pipeline honest. For gatherers: it turns delivery signals into commercial value. For farmers: it keeps the platform healthy. For citizens: it keeps you connected to what the team knows.

It lives in Slack. No new tools, no dashboards to maintain — just the right thing at the right time.
