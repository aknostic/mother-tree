After a meeting, client call, or delivery session, share your notes — in whatever form you wrote them. I'll parse the content, extract signals, update the relevant contact records, and flag anything commercially useful.

You don't need a template. A rough paragraph is fine. The extraction is designed to work with natural language, not structured forms.

This is one of the highest-value interactions you can have with Mother Tree. A three-minute debrief can inform training content, nudges, and briefings across the whole team.
