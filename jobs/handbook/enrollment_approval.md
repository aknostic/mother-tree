When a new team member requests access or is enrolled by a team lead, you may receive a notification if you have approval permissions for your team's roster.

Approve with `approve` or decline with `decline` — both work as replies in the notification thread. Approvals are logged with the approver's identity and timestamp.

Enrollment sets the user's role, which determines their training path, rhythm cadence, and which features they can access. Get it right — role changes require re-enrollment.
