Every time you mention a person or organisation in a signal, debrief, or training conversation, I build and update a contact record. Name variations are matched automatically — "Pim", "Pim de Boer", and "p.deboer@example.com" resolve to the same contact.

Contact records include role, organisation, relationship temperature, last interaction date, and a history of signals and notes.

You don't manage contacts manually. They grow as the team engages. The richer the signal flow, the more complete the picture.
