Before a meeting or call, ask me to brief you. I'll search across the full commercial intelligence layer — insights, signals, worldviews, personas — and return what's most relevant to your question.

Ask naturally: "Brief me on Consumentenbond ahead of Thursday" or "What do we know about infrastructure budget cycles in education?" The search is semantic, so you don't need exact keywords.

Briefings surface things you wouldn't think to look for. That's the point.
