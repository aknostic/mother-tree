When a meeting appears in your connected calendar, I prepare a brief two days before — relevant intelligence on the contact, recent signals, suggested angles, and anything from the training curriculum that applies.

Three days after the meeting, I'll prompt you for a debrief. Drop your notes into the thread and I'll extract the commercial content.

The calendar integration works with iCal. If your calendar isn't connected yet, ask your team lead to configure the feed. Once connected, prep and debrief happen automatically.
