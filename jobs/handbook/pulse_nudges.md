Pulse runs on weekday mornings. When a contact you care about has gone quiet — hot contacts after 7 days, warm contacts after 14 — I'll nudge you in Slack.

Each nudge includes context: what's been happening in their world, recent signals, and a suggested angle for reconnecting. Not a reminder to follow up. A reason to.

You can snooze a nudge if the timing is off, or engage directly and log the interaction. The clock resets when you do.
