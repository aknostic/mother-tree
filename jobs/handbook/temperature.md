Every contact has a temperature: hot (active, recent engagement), warm (engaged but quieter), or cold (significant gap, relationship at risk).

Temperature is calculated from interaction recency and quality — a meaningful conversation counts more than a forwarded email. Hot contacts get checked after 7 days of silence. Warm contacts after 14.

Temperature is the foundation of Pulse nudges. When a hot contact goes quiet, you hear about it. That's the system working as intended.
