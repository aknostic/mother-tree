- `next` — continue from where you left off, or start the next chapter
- `go` — jump straight into a session without preamble
- `practice` — repeat a scenario or concept you want to reinforce
- `status` — see your current chapter and streak
- `progress` — see your full curriculum progress

Training sessions adapt to your pace. If you drop off for a few days, I'll pick up where we left, not restart from scratch. Streaks track consistency, not speed.
