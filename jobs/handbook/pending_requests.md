To see any pending enrollment requests in your team, DM me `pending`. I'll list who's waiting, when they requested, and what role they're asking for.

Pending requests don't expire automatically — they stay open until approved or declined. If the list is getting long, it's usually a sign that someone in the approval chain has dropped the ball.

Admin visibility is role-gated. Only hunters with team lead permissions see pending requests from their teams.
