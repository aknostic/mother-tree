`help` returns a list of everything you can do, grouped by topic, filtered to your role. No irrelevant noise.

You can also ask in plain language — "how do I log a contact?" or "what does Pulse do?" — and I'll explain it. The help system is conversational, not just a command list.

In channels, @mention me and ask. In DM, just say it.
