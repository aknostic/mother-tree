# Independence Assessment — Assessment Template

> Template. Adapt to your domain and each engagement. Improve after each use.

## Overview

A time-boxed assessment that evaluates an organization's ability to operate independently of any single technology vendor, with focus on regulatory compliance and strategic autonomy.

## Phases

### Phase 1: Discovery

- Map technology dependencies across the organization
- Assess data residency and data flow patterns
- Interview stakeholders (security, technical leadership, compliance, procurement)
- Collect regulatory requirements and compliance deadlines
- Review existing vendor contracts and commitments

### Phase 2: Analysis

- Evaluate against applicable regulatory frameworks
- Assess supply chain control and visibility
- Map single points of vendor failure
- Evaluate data portability and exit feasibility
- Score independence across dimensions: infrastructure, data, skills, contracts, compliance

### Phase 3: Findings and roadmap

- Present findings as collaborative insights
- Independence scorecard with clear metrics
- Regulatory gap analysis with timeline to compliance
- Prioritized roadmap to increased independence
- Capability transfer plan

## Qualification signals to watch

See [qualification framework](../../methodology/qualification.md) for the three-phase read: pre-engagement, during, closure.

## After the assessment

- Log findings in Mother Tree (anonymized patterns feed the insight library)
- Conduct retrospective: what insights landed, what qualification signals did you read, what would you change
- If fit is confirmed, this naturally leads to a build engagement proposal
