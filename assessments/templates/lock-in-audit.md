# Lock-in Audit — Assessment Template

> Template. Adapt to your domain and each engagement. Improve after each use.

## Overview

A time-boxed assessment that quantifies a client's vendor lock-in exposure and maps their path to freedom to operate. Priced to signal investment, not cost.

## Phases

### Phase 1: Discovery

- Map current infrastructure, services, and vendor dependencies
- Identify proprietary dependencies (services, APIs, tooling, data formats)
- Interview key stakeholders (technical leadership, security, finance)
- Collect contract data (commitments, renewal dates, exit terms)

### Phase 2: Analysis

- Quantify switching costs per dependency
- Assess skill portability of current team
- Map regulatory exposure where applicable
- Calculate total cost of ownership including hidden costs
- Identify quick wins vs structural changes

### Phase 3: Findings and roadmap

- Present findings as collaborative insights (not a scolding)
- Quantified lock-in exposure with visual summary
- Prioritized roadmap: what to change, in what order, at what cost
- Capability transfer plan: what the client's team can do themselves vs where they need support

## Qualification signals to watch

See [qualification framework](../../methodology/qualification.md) for the three-phase read: pre-engagement, during, closure.

## After the assessment

- Log findings in Mother Tree (anonymized patterns feed the insight library)
- Conduct retrospective: what insights landed, what qualification signals did you read, what would you change
- If fit is confirmed, this naturally leads to a build engagement proposal
