## What is Mother Tree?

Mother Tree is our commercial intelligence platform. It connects everything we do — the relationships we build, the signals we pick up, the content we create, the assessments we deliver — into one system that gets smarter with every interaction.

Named after Suzanne Simard's research on how forests communicate underground. The biggest trees feed the seedlings. The network makes every tree stronger. That's what we're building.

It's not a CRM. It's the system that makes our way of selling actually work.

---

## Why we need this

We have three commercial strengths: reputation (publications, talks, credibility), stories (LinkedIn, thought leadership), and network (we know a lot of people, and people like us).

The problem: these three pillars generate warmth but not enough heat. We don't have a choreography that turns warmth into pipeline. Conversations stay friendly but never get to "here's something you don't know about your own risk." Or we get there, but there's no follow-through.

Mother Tree gives us the choreography, the tools, and the memory to make it systematic.

This is what makes us different from every other consultancy that quietly engineers switching costs. And it's what makes our selling authentic — we're not pretending. We actually believe this.

## Five principles

1. **Freedom to operate** — your systems should work for you. That starts with our relationship with you.

2. **Assessment is the first product** — we don't pitch and then sell. The first thing a client buys is clarity about their own situation. If the findings don't warrant working together, we part as friends.

3. **Qualify for mutual respect** — not every client is our client. We look for organizations where initiative and expertise are valued. We'd rather lose a deal early than win one that damages our people.

4. **Teach, don't pitch** — every interaction leaves the other person knowing something they didn't before. That's how reputation compounds.

5. **The network feeds the forest** — every signal, every lesson learned, every relationship makes the whole network smarter.

## Your role: Hunter

We have three roles in this system:

**Hunters** (you) — run the full commercial choreography. Turn signals into conversations, conversations into assessments, assessments into engagements. You decide when to pursue and when to walk away.

**Gatherers** — primarily in delivery, but everything they do generates fuel. Their client work creates reputation, their stories create content, their observations create signals. They hand things to you.

**Farmers** — keep the village running. The website, the tooling, the platform. Without farmers, hunters have no ground to stand on.

As a hunter, your superpower is relationships. You're likeable, people trust you, you build connections naturally. The methodology gives you structure for what to do with that trust — how to turn a good conversation into a teaching moment, and a teaching moment into an assessment.

## The choreography: how we sell

**Soil** — we exist in the landscape before anyone talks to us. Content, community, LinkedIn, reputation. Always running.

**Signal** — something moves. A gatherer hears something at a client. You spot a LinkedIn post. Someone reads an article you published. A regulation deadline approaches. Mother Tree captures it.

**Reframe** — the first real conversation. You share an insight they haven't considered. Not a pitch — a perspective shift. "Your multi-cloud strategy isn't multi-cloud — it's multi-vendor lock-in." This works because you're a practitioner, not a salesperson.

**Diagnosis** — they buy an assessment. Three weeks, EUR 40-50k. Lock-in audit, independence assessment, CTO retainer, or TCO analysis. This is real work with real value — and it's how we learn whether this is a client we want to work with.

**Proposal** — if the assessment confirms fit, we propose the build engagement (12-18 months). By now they trust us, they've seen our work, and the case was made by their own data.

**Sustain** — in production, 24/7 support follows naturally. And delivery feeds the whole cycle again — new signals, new stories, new reputation.

You don't always start at Signal. Some prospects arrive at Reframe (they already know us). Some jump to Diagnosis (they know they need an assessment). Pick up where the relationship already is.

## The assessment is the key

The assessment is both the first product and the deepest qualification gate. While you deliver value, you read three sets of signals:

**Before:** How do they negotiate the price? Hard haggling means they see us as vendors, not partners. Scope squeezing ("do it in one week for half") is a walk-away signal.

**During:** Do they make time? Are they open? Is initiative welcome, or does it feel like encroachment? Are there signs of dysfunction — blame culture, politics, toxicity?

**After:** Do findings land as collaborative insights, or does it feel like a scolding? Do they have energy to act, or does the report go in a drawer?

If the signals are wrong, we part as friends. The assessment delivered value regardless. "We qualified for mutual respect" is better than "we lost the deal."

The library grows. Every assessment, every conversation, every pattern we spot adds to it. What you learn in the field comes back and makes every hunter sharper.

## Training: Mother Tree makes you sharper

Mother Tree isn't just a database. It actively helps you develop as a consultative seller through a structured, role-specific curriculum.

**Your training path (5 stages):**
1. **The Foundation** — the change, the worldview, the audience (Saga teaches)
2. **The Offering** — connecting change to evidence (Saga teaches)
3. **The Conversation** — reframes, objection handling, stakeholder lenses (Lena teaches)
4. **The Difference** — competitive positioning, why us (Saga teaches)
5. **The Hunt** — full choreography in practice (Lena teaches)

Each stage has chapters with exercises generated from real content in the central intelligence. The system tracks your proficiency per chapter — if knowledge decays, you get targeted refreshers.

**Daily interaction** — via Slack DM. `next` for the next chapter, `go` to start exercises, `practice [topic]` to drill a specific area. Answer with A, B, or C. Takes 5 minutes. Tracks streaks. `status` to see where you are.

**Conversation prep** — before a meeting, Mother Tree pulls what it knows about the contact and company, suggests relevant reframes, and asks: "What's your goal for this meeting?" After the meeting: "How did it go? Drop your notes here." Your notes become intelligence that makes the next conversation better.

## Rhythm: Mother Tree keeps you sharp

**Every weekday morning (08:00):** relationship check — hot contacts without interaction in 7 days, warm contacts in 14 days. You get a Slack DM with who needs attention. Meeting prep for anything coming up in 2 days.

**After meetings (3 days):** debrief prompt if no notes were captured. Quick notes, Mother Tree handles the rest.

**Every Monday (08:00):** pipeline review. Where is each conversation? What needs attention? What's gone cold?

**1st of every month (09:00):** monthly retrospective — LLM-synthesized pipeline health, insight usage patterns, training progress.

**Always:** follow-up reminders when conversations go stale. Calendar-aware prep and debrief via iCal sync.
