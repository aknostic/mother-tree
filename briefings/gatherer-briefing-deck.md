## What is Mother Tree?

Mother Tree is our commercial intelligence platform. It connects everything we do — the relationships we build, the signals we pick up, the content we create, the assessments we deliver — into one system that gets smarter with every interaction.

Named after Suzanne Simard's research on how forests communicate underground. The biggest trees feed the seedlings. The network makes every tree stronger. That's what we're building.

It's not a CRM. It's the system that makes our way of selling actually work.

---

## Why we need this

We have three commercial strengths: reputation (publications, talks, credibility), stories (LinkedIn, thought leadership), and network (we know a lot of people, and people like us).

The problem: these three pillars generate warmth but not enough heat. Individually, none of them converts reliably into pipeline. Mother Tree connects them — and your role is at the heart of that connection.

## The core message

**"You keep us because you want to, not because you have to."**

This is our version of freedom to operate — applied to our own client relationships. We don't create dependency. We transfer capability. Clients stay because they choose to, not because they're locked in.

The proof is in long-term client relationships — clients who stay for years, grow with you, and ask you to help plan their future. They stay because working with you makes everything resilient and reliable. Every piece of great delivery work you do extends that track record — and it's the strongest sales argument we have.

## Five principles

1. **Freedom to operate** — your systems should work for you. That starts with our relationship with you.

2. **Assessment is the first product** — we don't pitch and then sell. The first thing a client buys is clarity about their own situation.

3. **Qualify for mutual respect** — not every client is our client. We look for organizations where initiative and expertise are valued.

4. **Teach, don't pitch** — every interaction leaves the other person knowing something they didn't before. That's how reputation compounds.

5. **The network feeds the forest** — every signal, every lesson learned, every relationship makes the whole network smarter.

## Your role: Gatherer

We have three roles in this system:

**Hunters** — run the full commercial choreography. Turn signals into conversations, conversations into assessments, assessments into engagements.

**Gatherers** (you) — visible in the field, bringing back commercial fuel. Your delivery work, your stories, your observations are the raw material that makes everything else possible.

**Farmers** — keep the village running. The website, the tooling, the platform.

As a gatherer, your superpower is that you're where the work happens. You see what clients actually struggle with. You hear what keeps their teams up at night. You build relationships with people who trust you because you've solved their problems. That's intelligence no amount of LinkedIn monitoring can replace.

---

## What gatherers do

You don't need to sell. You need to notice things and share them. Specifically:

**Spot signals during delivery.** A client mentions their sister company is struggling with cloud migration. A team lead complains about vendor lock-in. Someone asks if you know anyone who can help with NIS2 compliance. A new VP joins with a mandate to modernize. These are signals.

**Share what you see.** Drop a Slack message: "Talked to X at client Y, they mentioned Z." That's it. Mother Tree picks it up, enriches it, and routes it to the right hunter. You don't need to qualify it or write a report. Just share the raw observation.

**Create stories.** Your delivery work generates content. The pattern you noticed across three clients. The migration approach that saved six months. The architectural decision that unlocked a team. Write it up for LinkedIn, your company blog, or a talk. Every story you tell builds the reputation that brings the next client in.

**Build relationships beyond your project scope.** The people you work with know other people. The CTO you delivered for has a peer at another company. The conference you spoke at has attendees who remember your talk. These connections are the network — and they compound.

## How it flows

When you spot a signal, here's what happens:

1. You say something in any Slack channel — a client mention, a meeting observation, a pattern you noticed
2. The Spotter (an invisible character) extracts entities, signals, actions, and sales stage
3. The Weaver resolves those into the relationship graph — deduplicating contacts, linking people to companies
4. If it's commercially relevant, it surfaces to the right hunter
5. The hunter decides whether to pursue it
6. If it becomes an engagement, you might hear back: "that lead from your client turned into an assessment"

You close the loop. Your signal became an opportunity. That pattern repeats.

## The insight library — you feed it

Hunters use insights to start conversations. But insights come from delivery — from what you see working with clients.

When you notice a pattern, share it:
- "Every financial services client we work with hits the same DORA compliance wall in month three"
- "Teams migrating from AWS to Kubernetes consistently underestimate the IAM transition"
- "Platform teams that use vendor-specific tooling spend 40% of their time on maintenance instead of building"

These observations become reframes that hunters use in the next conversation. Your field knowledge is the ammunition.

## The tools: what changes for you

Almost nothing changes in your daily work. You keep delivering, keep learning, keep building relationships. The difference is that now there's a system that catches what you share and makes it useful.

**What you'll actually do differently:**
- When you spot something interesting, drop it in Slack instead of just mentioning it at the next standup (or forgetting it entirely)
- When you write content — a LinkedIn post, a talk, an article — know that it feeds the Soil stage of our sales choreography
- After an assessment engagement, you might get a prompt asking what you learned. Five minutes of reflection that feeds the whole system

**What you don't need to do:**
- No CRM data entry
- No pipeline management
- No sales calls (unless you want to)
- No learning a new tool — Slack and email are your interface

## What happens next

**Week 1:** We set up Mother Tree. Connect Slack. Start sharing signals as you normally would — the system starts capturing and enriching them.

**Week 2-4:** Build the habit. When you hear something at a client, when you notice a pattern, when someone mentions a name — share it. Short, raw, unfiltered. The system handles the rest.

**Month 2:** By now, some of your signals will have turned into real conversations or assessments. You'll see the loop closing — your observations becoming pipeline.

**Ongoing:** Every story you tell, every signal you share, every pattern you notice makes Mother Tree smarter. When Cell-2 spins up somewhere in Europe, the new team inherits everything we've collectively learned.

Your work already has commercial value. Mother Tree just makes sure that value doesn't get lost.
