# Regulatory Pressure

Insights about compliance deadlines, regulatory requirements, and the gap between current state and what regulators expect. Applicable to any consultancy in a regulated market.

## What belongs here

- Reframes that reveal regulatory exposure the prospect hasn't considered
- Evidence based on specific regulations, enforcement timelines, or audit requirements
- Triggers: upcoming deadlines, enforcement actions, new legislation, sector-specific requirements

## Stakeholder tailoring

- **Security/compliance:** Frame as compliance risk — specific articles, deadlines, evidence requirements
- **Finance:** Frame as regulatory fine exposure — quantify the cost of non-compliance
- **Technical leadership:** Frame as technical requirement — what does compliance mean architecturally
- **Executive:** Frame as board-level governance risk

## Example insight structure

```yaml
category: regulatory-pressure
reframe: "Can you demonstrate supply chain control to your regulator by the deadline?"
evidence: "Regulation X Article Y requires risk management measures including supply chain security."
stakeholder_lens:
  - role: CISO
    framing: "The audit is in 6 months. Can you produce the evidence?"
  - role: CEO
    framing: "Non-compliance is a board-level governance failure."
trigger: "Upcoming regulatory deadline or audit"
next_step: "Assessment scoped to regulatory readiness"
```
