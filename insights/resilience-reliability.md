# Resilience and Reliability

Insights about operational maturity, long-term client relationships, and building systems that last. Applicable to any consultancy with a strong delivery track record.

## What belongs here

- Reframes that use track record as proof of the delivery model
- Evidence based on client retention data, system uptime, or growth over time
- Triggers: prospect asks about references, longevity, or operational risk

## Stakeholder tailoring

- **Technical leadership:** Frame as engineering maturity — resilient systems require less heroics
- **Finance:** Frame as predictability — resilient systems have more predictable cost profiles
- **Executive:** Frame as business continuity — the difference between surviving disruption and not

## Example insight structure

```yaml
category: resilience-reliability
reframe: "Our longest client relationship is N years. They keep choosing us — not because they can't leave."
evidence: "Client X: N years, continuous growth, now planning future together."
stakeholder_lens:
  - role: CTO
    framing: "We build systems that outlast the team that built them."
  - role: CEO
    framing: "Resilience is a business outcome, not a technical metric."
trigger: "Prospect asks about track record or references"
next_step: "Any assessment — the track record supports all entry points"
```
