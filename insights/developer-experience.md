# Developer Experience

Insights about tooling lock-in, hiring constraints, knowledge transfer barriers, and developer productivity. Applicable to any consultancy working with engineering teams.

## What belongs here

- Reframes about how tooling choices affect developer productivity and mobility
- Evidence based on onboarding time, hiring data, developer satisfaction, or skill transferability
- Triggers: growing teams, high turnover, slow onboarding, developer satisfaction surveys

## Stakeholder tailoring

- **Technical leadership:** Frame as team velocity — portable tooling means faster everything
- **Engineering management:** Frame as developer satisfaction — engineers prefer open standards
- **Finance:** Frame as hiring cost — the hidden cost of vendor-specific skills
- **Executive:** Frame as organizational agility — adaptable teams are a competitive advantage

## Example insight structure

```yaml
category: developer-experience
reframe: "Your developer onboarding time tells you how locked in you are."
evidence: "Open-standards onboarding transfers across organizations; vendor-specific does not."
stakeholder_lens:
  - role: CTO
    framing: "How long until a new hire is productive? That's your lock-in score."
  - role: VP Engineering
    framing: "Your best engineers want to work with tools they can take to their next job."
trigger: "Hiring difficulties or slow onboarding"
next_step: "Developer experience assessment"
```
