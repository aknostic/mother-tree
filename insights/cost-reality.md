# Cost Reality

Insights about total cost of ownership, hidden costs, and the gap between what clients think they pay and what they actually pay. Applicable to any consultancy offering cost optimization or platform alternatives.

## What belongs here

- Reframes that reveal hidden or long-term costs the prospect hasn't calculated
- Evidence based on TCO analysis, contract terms, or real migration data
- Triggers: budget reviews, contract renewals, pricing increases, cost optimization initiatives

## Stakeholder tailoring

- **Finance:** Frame as financial risk — show the full TCO trajectory including hidden costs
- **Technical leadership:** Frame as technical debt with a price tag — vendor-specific choices compound
- **Procurement:** Frame as negotiating leverage — understanding true costs changes the power dynamic
- **Executive:** Frame as business model risk — cost structures that grow faster than revenue are threats

## Example insight structure

```yaml
category: cost-reality
reframe: "Your bill is the price of admission. The real cost is what you cannot see."
evidence: "Hidden costs include: egress fees, support tiers, committed spend lock-in, training costs."
stakeholder_lens:
  - role: CFO
    framing: "Show me your 5-year TCO including switching costs."
  - role: CTO
    framing: "Every architectural shortcut has a compounding cost."
trigger: "Budget review or contract renewal"
next_step: "TCO analysis that includes hidden and long-term costs"
```
