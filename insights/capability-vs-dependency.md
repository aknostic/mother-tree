# Capability vs Dependency

Insights about team productivity, skill portability, and the difference between building internal capability and creating vendor dependency. Applicable to any consultancy focused on knowledge transfer.

## What belongs here

- Reframes that reveal how vendor-specific tooling limits team capability
- Evidence based on team productivity metrics, hiring data, or skill transferability
- Triggers: hiring difficulties, onboarding time, team scaling challenges, platform team reviews

## Stakeholder tailoring

- **Technical leadership:** Frame as team velocity — portable skills mean faster hiring, onboarding, and delivery
- **Engineering management:** Frame as talent retention — engineers prefer working with transferable skills
- **Finance:** Frame as hiring cost — vendor-specific skills in a tight market cost more
- **Executive:** Frame as organizational resilience — teams that can adapt quickly are a competitive advantage

## Example insight structure

```yaml
category: capability-vs-dependency
reframe: "Your team is maintaining vendor-specific tooling instead of building portable skills."
evidence: "Teams on proprietary tooling spend X% of time on vendor-specific maintenance."
stakeholder_lens:
  - role: CTO
    framing: "Every vendor-specific hire is harder to find and harder to replace."
  - role: CFO
    framing: "What percentage of your platform budget is vendor-specific maintenance?"
trigger: "Platform team scaling challenges or hiring difficulties"
next_step: "Assessment of current team capability and portability"
```
